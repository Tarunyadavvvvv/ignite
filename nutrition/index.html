Nutrition Fundamentals

Training gets you there — nutrition decides how fast, and how well you recover along the way. Start with the fundamentals below, then pick the goal that matches yours.

The building blocks

Macronutrients

Everything you eat breaks down into three macronutrients. Getting the balance right matters more than any single "superfood."

P

Protein

Repairs and builds muscle tissue. Aim for roughly 1.6-2.2g per kg of bodyweight daily if training regularly.

C

Carbohydrates

Your primary training fuel, especially for higher-intensity or longer sessions.

F

Fats

Supports hormone production, joint health and long-duration energy.

⏱️

Calories

The energy balance that ultimately decides whether you gain, lose or maintain weight — everything else is refinement on top of this.

🥦

Whole Foods First

Build meals around minimally processed proteins, carbohydrates and fats — supplements and "extras" fill gaps, they don't replace this.

🔁

Consistency Over Perfection

A good-enough plan followed consistently outperforms a perfect plan you abandon after a week.
