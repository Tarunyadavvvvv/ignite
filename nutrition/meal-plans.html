Sample Meal Plans

These are starting templates, not rigid prescriptions — swap in foods you actually enjoy and can source easily, while keeping the overall protein/carb/fat structure similar.

Sample Day — Muscle Gain
  • Breakfast: Oats with Greek yogurt, banana and a scoop of protein powder
  • Lunch: Chicken breast, rice and mixed vegetables
  • Pre-Workout Snack: Toast with peanut butter and a piece of fruit
  • Post-Workout: Whey protein shake with a banana
  • Dinner: Salmon or lean beef, potatoes and a side salad
  • Evening Snack: Cottage cheese with nuts
Sample Day — Fat Loss
  • Breakfast: Egg whites with vegetables and one whole egg
  • Lunch: Grilled chicken salad with a light dressing
  • Snack: Greek yogurt with berries
  • Dinner: White fish, steamed vegetables and a small portion of rice
  • Evening Snack (optional): A piece of fruit or a small handful of nuts
Sample Day — Maintenance
  • Breakfast: Whole grain toast with eggs and avocado
  • Lunch: A grain bowl with a protein of choice and mixed vegetables
  • Snack: Fruit and a small handful of nuts
  • Dinner: A balanced plate — protein, whole-food carb, vegetables and a fat source
  • Flexible Slot: Room for a food you enjoy, in a sensible portion
This is a starting framework. A detailed, goal-specific meal plan database with exact portions and calorie counts is planned as a future addition — for now, use these as adaptable daily templates.
