The IGNITE system

One system. Eleven phases.

A complete session — and a complete beginner path — follows the same practical sequence real coaches use.

01 🔥

Warm-Up

Raise your heart rate and prep your joints before anything intense.

 02 🤸

Mobility & Activation

Unlock range of motion and switch on the muscles you're about to use.

 03 💪

Home / Bodyweight

Zero-equipment strength training you can do anywhere.

 04 🏋️

Gym / Weighted

Barbell, dumbbell and machine lifts to build real strength.

 05 🎯

Core Training

Build a stable, bulletproof midline that protects every other lift.

 06 🏃

Cardio

Conditioning work that builds endurance and torches energy.

 07 🌬️

Cool-Down

Bring your heart rate back down before you stretch or leave.

 08 🙆

Stretching

Static holds that restore flexibility and ease muscle tightness.
09

Yoga

Breath-led flows for balance, control and a calmer nervous system.

 10 🌙

Recovery & Rest

Sleep, rest days and active recovery — where the real growth happens.

 11 🥗

Diet / Nutrition

Fuel the work — macros, meal timing and food strategy that fits your goal.
